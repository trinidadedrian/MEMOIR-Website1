Game Overview

Moving out is pretty straightforward, or is it really that simple?

Rikka’s outgrown her old shabby apartment and it’s time to move out, the cramped space, the chipping walls, and the faint smell of old flooring has all played a part of who she is today.

But something seems amiss, fragments of the past flash through her mind as the days go by, an uneasy feeling fills the air in her apartment, a feeling you can’t ignore forever, a feeling that she must unravel before she can truly leave the apartment.

Characters

RIKKA

A seemingly normal lady who needs to move out of her apartment, strangely enough she has an unclear memory of her life events a few weeks before moving out. She also experiences bizarre dreams and sometimes realistically vivid nightmares.
